Beyond Analytics

The other sides of me - hobbies, passions, and what makes me tick

While I love diving deep into data and solving complex problems, I believe that being a well-rounded person makes me a better analyst. Here's what I do when I'm not crunching numbers or building models.

📚

Learning & Reading

I'm constantly reading about emerging technologies, business trends, and new analytical techniques. I believe that staying curious and continuously learning is essential in the fast-paced world of data analytics.

👥

Leadership

I'm actively involved in student organizations and academic initiatives. Leading teams has taught me invaluable lessons about collaboration, communication, and empathy.

  • Event Lead, Campus to Career session series, UCR School of Business
  • Team Lead, Simulation for Business Project: Led a three-person AI project on valuation risk, ensuring timely results and bringing out each team member’s strengths.
🤝

Community & Volunteering

Giving back to the community is important to me. I enjoy creating meaningful learning experiences and supporting others as they grow.

  • Teaching Assistant, Standard Chartered Bank: Conducted Personal Financial Management training sessions for students at Hung Vuong University, helping them build practical money management skills and financial awareness.
  • Customer Relations Executive, AIESEC in Vietnam: Supported international exchange participants by sharing essential information, addressing concerns, and organizing engagement activities to ensure a positive volunteering experience abroad.
🏃

Walking & Thinking

I value the quiet moments in between - walking after class or on my way to lunch or dinner. Walking helps me process the day: what I’ve done, what I could improve, and what to focus on next. Watching people pass and looking up at the sky brings me calm and perspective. These simple walks ground me. They help me reset, feel grateful, think clearly, and move forward with purpose.

🎨

Listening to Music & Singing

I usually start my day with a favorite or energetic song. The lyrics often help me make sense of thoughts that feel blurry or hard to describe. Singing along brings a special kind of joy - it relaxes me, lifts my mood, and makes me feel connected, even in moments when I am alone at home. Music grounds me, gives clarity, and reminds me of simple happiness in everyday life.

🌍

Cooking

I spend a lot of time in the kitchen because cooking feels like another language of love. It’s a way for me to be creative and put care into something that nourishes both myself and the people I care about. I love Vietnamese food and enjoy making dishes like Pho, Bun Bo Hue (Hue Beef Noodle Soup), and Vietnamese Broken Rice with Grilled Pork. Cooking these dishes connects me to my roots, and each meal feels like a small celebration of effort and flavor. I look forward to spending more time experimenting in the kitchen and sharing my creations soon.

Why This Matters

I believe that being a great data analyst isn't just about technical skills. It's about understanding people, communicating insights effectively, staying curious, and bringing diverse experiences to the table. These activities and interests make me a more empathetic, creative, and well-rounded professional.
