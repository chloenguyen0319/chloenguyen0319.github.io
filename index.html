About Me

A bit about my journey...

I started my career at Standard Chartered thinking I'd build a future in banking. But somewhere between loyalty program redesigns and biometric feature launches, I realized something: the most exciting part of my day wasn't the transaction, it was understanding the patterns behind them. While my colleagues focused on execution, I found myself asking "what if we analyzed this differently?"

That curiosity brought me from Ho Chi Minh City to UC Riverside, where I'm pursuing my MSBA. The transition wasn't easy, but it confirmed what I suspected. I'm genuinely energized by complex datasets and the stories they reveal. Give me messy data and ambiguous questions, and I'll work until I find insights that move the needle.

What drives me isn't just producing reports or running models. It's being the analyst who makes data accessible and actionable, who can walk into a room, present findings clearly, and help teams make confident decisions. I want to bridge the gap between raw numbers and real business impact.
